NurseApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026.

NurseApp lets you record health measurements and organize medication reminders. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and what happens when you export or delete it.

1. Developer and contact

NurseApp is developed by Ricardo Pájaro Coatl and published as NullPointerDev. For privacy questions or requests concerning your data, contact nullpointerdeveloper@gmail.com.

2. Records you enter

The app stores the glucose, blood pressure, temperature, and blood oxygen measurements you enter manually, together with their dates, in its local storage. It also stores medication names, doses, comments, schedules, reminder duration, photo references, and recorded responses about doses. History may include corrections and alarm activity.

These records are used to display your measurements and charts, organize reminders, show history, and generate exports you request. Marking a dose as taken is a statement by the user; it does not establish that the medication was actually taken.

NurseApp does not require an app account and does not provide a feature to synchronize these records with a developer-operated server. This does not mean that all technical information stays exclusively on your device: the services described below may transmit information.

3. Photos

You can choose an image using the system picker or open a camera app. NurseApp accesses the image you select without requesting general access to your entire photo library. Photos taken for reminders are stored in the app's cache and may be removed by the operating system. Original images selected from your photo library remain outside NurseApp.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, Google services used to analyze app usage and diagnose errors. These services may transmit usage events, installation identifiers, app version, device and operating system information, and error reports. Analytics may derive approximate location from an IP address; this does not involve GPS access or precise location.

Error reports may contain exception messages and technical context, including the type of measurement involved. No dedicated feature has been identified that uploads your measurement values or photos to a developer-operated server. However, we cannot guarantee that error messages never contain sensitive context.

These services are enabled by default in the reviewed version. NurseApp currently does not provide a switch to disable them. Records and reminders can be used offline; when a connection is available, technical services may transmit information.

Google may process information using international infrastructure under its terms and policies. See Firebase privacy and security information and Google's Privacy Policy. Service configurations and versions may affect how data is processed.

5. Data retention

Local records remain in the app's storage until you delete them using the available controls or clear that storage. Not every type of history has an in-app deletion control.

The reviewed Analytics property is configured to retain event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months. Resetting the user retention period on new activity is enabled, so that period may be renewed. These controls do not affect most standard aggregated reports. See Analytics data retention for more information.

According to Google's documentation, Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting their removal from live and backup systems. Remote deletion is not necessarily immediate, and different services may follow different retention periods.

6. Android backups and device transfers

Versions implementing NurseApp's new privacy rules exclude the database containing measurements, medications, and history from new automatic Android backups and device transfers. General settings may remain eligible for backup. Sensitive records will not be automatically restored through these backups when you change devices.

Earlier versions allowed this database to be included in system backups. Changing the rules does not automatically delete existing backups. Manage older backups through Android settings or the relevant backup provider.

A PDF is a report, not a restorable app backup. CSV exports contain measurements and are not complete backups of medications, photos, and history.

7. Exports and support

PDFs, CSV files, and diagnostic reports are generated when you request them. They may contain health information. Diagnostic reports may include medication names, alarm activity, and technical traces. Review a file before sharing it.

You choose where to save files or whom to share them with using the system's options. Recipients and apps you select have their own privacy practices. Avoid sending unnecessary medical information when requesting support.

8. Permissions and security

Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen presentation, vibration, and the playback service support reminders when Android permits them. Wake-lock access helps maintain an alert, and receiving boot events allows alarms to be rescheduled. Internet access supports the technical services described above.

You can manage permissions in Android settings. Denied permissions, battery restrictions, and other system conditions may prevent an alert. Do not rely on NurseApp as your only way to remember a critical dose.

Local records are protected by Android's private app storage mechanisms; we do not claim that the local database has additional app-specific encryption. Google documents HTTPS connections for Firebase services. The security of exported files also depends on where you save and share them. No system guarantees absolute security.

9. Deletion and privacy requests

You can delete measurements using their controls and delete reminders individually. The action to delete all measurements does not necessarily delete reminders or all history. Clearing NurseApp's storage or uninstalling the app removes its private local data.

These actions do not automatically delete exported files, original photos in your library, older system backups, or information already sent to Google. Manage external files in their respective locations. For questions about data processed by the developer, or access or deletion requests, use the contact email above.

Available actions depend on what information can be identified and applicable obligations. The app does not promise to automatically associate an installation without an account with a particular person or immediately erase all remote data. If you contact support, your email address and message contents will be processed to address your request.

10. Children's privacy and intended audience

NurseApp is intended for people aged 13 or older and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information to the developer, contact us using the email above so that we can assess appropriate action. This intended audience does not change the content rating displayed by Google Play.

11. Health disclaimer and policy updates

NurseApp is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. It does not take measurements using sensors and does not replace professional medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional about your measurements and medication; reference values are informational only.

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with their date. Material changes to data processing must also be reflected in the app and Google Play disclosures.
